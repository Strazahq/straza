Straza / the integration loop · who vs. what

The IdM decides WHO, Straza decides WHAT, the pull closes the audit loop. Press “grant access” to follow a membership from business role to running session.
What this shows

The whole identity model in one loop. A person or agent gets access by holding a business role in the IdM: the SOURCE grant, requested and revoked by the joiner/mover/leaver machinery the enterprise already trusts. That assignment flows over SCIM as membership of the wire-group rendering role dev, and that membership IS the Straza role assignment. What the role MEANS is authored on the Straza side only: bindings decide what its sessions can see, policy decides allow/deny/needs-a-human, and admin.roleAreas decides what it may administer, so the IdM can decide who is dev, but can never conjure what dev can do (nor create, rename or delete the role: it is Straza-born). The pull direction imports Straza’s runtime truth into the IdM (AI: roles, MCS: apps, MCT: tools) so a certification campaign over the source grant has real evidence to read: the reviewer opens AI:dev and certifies a capability set, not a label. Certifying or revoking an app or tool mirror would remediate nothing: what bites is the source grant, and under it the imported role assignment that writes membership, which is exactly why the campaign targets the grant. A Straza-side assignment with no matching IdM grant carries origin: admin and is the drift tripwire’s business: legitimate for bootstrap, break-glass and IdM-less standalone deployments, visible in every case, and reconciled away on the IdM’s next run, since SCIM is the single writer for an exported role.
