Straza / IGA LiveSync loop

Two lanes, loop-safe by direction: identity flows in over SCIM; catalog changes flow back over the cursored feed. Press “deploy an app” to watch a create ripple into midPoint.
What this shows

The bidirectional IGA loop. midPoint owns users and writes them into Straza over the SCIM resource only, together with the one other writable thing: membership of the wire-groups that render Straza's exported roles, which IS the role assignment. In the other direction Straza never receives writes: the universal-rest pull resource is strictly read-only, and universal-rest-connector implements ConnId SyncOp over GET /v1/admin/changes: an ordered page over the events outbox with a uuidv7 cursor, at-least-once delivery (consumers dedupe; periodic recon stays the safety net), and hard deletes surfaced as tombstone markers. midPoint LiveSync tasks poll every 10 seconds and materialize the catalog: apps become ServiceType foci (archetype mcp-server), tools become RoleType foci named app:tool (mcp-tool), exported Straza roles become RoleType foci (ai-role) named AI:<role>. Those role foci are assignable: assigning one writes membership back over SCIM, so the grant is real. Users are link-only (creating a focus for a Straza-local account would immediately provision it back over SCIM, a loop). Deleting an app in Straza leaves a dead shadow in midPoint, and the deleted > deleteFocus reaction removes the focus. Cursor values shown are illustrative.
