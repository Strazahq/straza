A change to MCP servers, roles, access rows or policy sets can go through a draft, which strazad checks against live state and a person then publishes in one step.

- [Who may draft and publish]({{< relref "guides/changes/who-may-draft.md" >}}) decides who may draft a change, who publishes it, and when a second person must.

- [Propose and publish a change]({{< relref "guides/changes/propose-and-publish.md" >}}) creates, checks, publishes and reverts a draft.
