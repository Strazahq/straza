The console lives at `/console/` on your server, and the root address redirects to `/self-service/`. Answering held calls in the console is under [Approvals]({{< relref "guides/approve/_index.md" >}}), and drafts are under [Changes and drafts]({{< relref "guides/changes/_index.md" >}}).

- [Overview and Audit]({{< relref "guides/console/overview-and-audit.md" >}}) checks the audit chain, follows the live tail and searches records.
- [Roles]({{< relref "guides/console/roles.md" >}}) walks a role's page and its tabs.
- [Policies]({{< relref "guides/console/policies.md" >}}) builds a policy with the New policy wizard and tests a call.
- [Settings]({{< relref "guides/console/settings.md" >}}) reads the configuration and keeps the attestation registry.
