- [Approve in the console]({{< relref "guides/approve/console.md" >}}) answers a held call from the console and watches the retry run.
- [Slack]({{< relref "guides/approve/slack.md" >}}) routes held calls to a Slack channel and answers them there.
- [Approve on a phone]({{< relref "guides/approve/phone.md" >}}) enrolls a phone as an approver, so Straza sends the request to the approver's phone and the answer is signed on the device.
- [Push delivery and connectivity]({{< relref "guides/approve/push-and-connectivity.md" >}}) gives the approver listener an address every phone can open and chooses how push reaches the phones.

- [Approve in the browser]({{< relref "guides/approve/browser.md" >}}) lets a person who is not an admin approve from the self-service page.
